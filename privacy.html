Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

1. Overview

This policy describes how StreamerClipsBot handles information when users operate the Windows desktop application.

2. Information processed

Depending on how the application is configured, it may process Twitch channel names, livestream content, video clips, subtitles, local file paths, and application logs. Twitch or TikTok authentication may involve account identifiers and access tokens.

3. Purpose and storage

Information is processed to detect streams, create clips, add subtitles, and prepare authorized uploads. Video files and logs may be stored locally on the user's computer. Data sent to Twitch or TikTok is also subject to their respective privacy policies.

4. Sharing with third parties

The application may communicate with Twitch and TikTok when the user enables related features. Information is sent only as necessary for those features. This policy must be updated if additional services or analytics are introduced.

5. Retention and deletion

Locally stored recordings, clips, and logs can be deleted by the user. The actual retention period depends on the user's settings and actions. Data retained by third-party platforms is governed by their policies.

6. Security

Users should protect their credentials and access tokens and should not share them publicly. No software can guarantee absolute security.
